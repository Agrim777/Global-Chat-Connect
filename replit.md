# Global Chat Connect

Anonymous human-to-human Telegram text chat for adults, with safety-first defaults.

## Safety commitments

- No AI chat, fake persona, automated impersonation, or engagement bait.
- Mandatory 18+ confirmation, Terms/Disclaimer and Privacy Policy consent before signup.
- Gender is self-declared and locked after signup; the bot never guarantees gender or matching.
- Female accounts are free; male accounts require Telegram Stars access: 150 Stars / 2 weeks, 250 Stars / 1 month, 1000 Stars / lifetime.
- Text only. Photos, videos, files, voice notes, contacts, locations, and stickers are blocked.
- Instagram/personal-DM contact requests are blocked with an immediate scam warning.
- Users can report partners; repeated reports alert the admin for review.
- Female profile completions notify `ADMIN_TELEGRAM_ID`.

## Runtime variables

Required: `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`, `DATABASE_URL`.

Recommended: `ADMIN_TELEGRAM_ID`, `BOT_POLLING_ENABLED=true`.
